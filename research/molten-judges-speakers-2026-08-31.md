## 4. How to ask (top candidates)

**Debevec:** Short, specific, technical. Name the CAPTURE track and that teams will ship
volumetric/reconstruction demos in 24h; ask for exactly 2 hours Saturday afternoon as a
judge, Friday talk only as an alternative. Mention the a16z/LA Tech Week frame (Fil
confirmed naming Tech Week helps) and that USC ICT is already engaged — his old lab.
Do NOT ask for anything else (no sponsorship, no hardware); a single clean ask to a
gmail he publishes on purpose.

**Yajie Zhao:** Recruiting frame: 120 screened technical students from USC/UCLA/Caltech,
several of whom will apply to PhD programs in exactly her area; judging CAPTURE/INHABIT
puts her in front of them first. One paragraph on what teams will build (splats, scene
reconstruction, avatars). Cc nothing; keep it separate from the MxR thread but mention
MOLTEN named ICT in the a16z application.

**Todorovic:** Open on democratizing 3D creation for people without VFX training — his
exact public line — and that every team ships a spatial build in 24h. Ask: judge Saturday
or 15-min Friday talk. LinkedIn DM first (no verified email); short enough to read on a
phone.

**Karamian:** Two-part ask in one email: (a) would VRARA LA co-promote MOLTEN as a
community partner, (b) would he personally judge Saturday. Lead with (b) — the personal
ask is the flattering one and the chapter partnership rides along. Reference the chapter's
event program so it's clearly not a form letter.

**Kostopoulos:** Peer-to-peer tone, not sponsor tone — he is barely older than the
attendees. Open on Body of Mine (INHABIT track is his lane), ask him to judge or mentor,
and separately ask if he'd point Epic DevRel and the LA Unreal chapter at the event. He
is simultaneously a judge candidate AND the missing Epic route from `outreach-drafts.md`.

**Fisher:** Frame as historical bookend: Project Holodeck's USC lineage is already in the
MxR draft; Fisher predates all of it. Ask for a 20-minute Friday opener on what the first
VR generation got wrong and right. Faculty answer email; `sfisher@cinema.usc.edu` is
published.

**de la Peña:** Hook is the LA fires Gaussian-splat capture project — her team, LA
subject matter, and the exact technique the CAPTURE track uses. Offer Friday talk or a
capture masterclass, and invite her NEM grad students to attend. (Charlie Fink teaches in
her program — one warm reply can yield the other.)

**Crowder:** USC-alum angle plus "we need technical judges, and your ADC judging + 14
graphics patents are exactly the profile." Offer either seat: Friday talk or Saturday
