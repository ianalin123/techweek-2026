## MOLTEN

A 120-person, 24-hour a16z Tech Week hackathon for spatial creation.
LA, Saturday October 17 to Sunday October 18, 2026.

## The thesis

Spatial computing keeps shipping better hardware than software. The best headset in the
world still runs a floating browser. MOLTEN pushes 120 builders through a 24-hour sprint
to ship spatial output that actually uses the body as an input device.

## The rules

Every submission must:

1. Ship a spatial output (a 3D world, a scene, a persistent layer, a physical build)
2. Use at least two body-input modalities (hand, eye, voice, EMG, pose, controller,
   camera-based)

## Tracks

- **WORLDBUILD** — generate or compose 3D worlds
- **NAVIGATE** — new ways to move through spatial content
- **CAPTURE** — scanning, reconstruction, spatial memory
- **INHABIT** — presence, avatars, shared spatial state
- **OPEN** — anything spatial that breaks the pattern

## Attendees

120 hackers. Students and within 2 to 3 years of graduation. Technical and creative.
Screened application. USC and UCLA core with Berkeley pipeline and LA XR studio alumni.
