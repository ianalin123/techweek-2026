# MOLTEN venue outreach (Peerspace-sourced)

Built 2026-08-27. Sourced from Peerspace LA, filtered to capacity 120+, then each venue
identified off-platform so we can email and call direct instead of booking through
Peerspace.

## The hours problem, read this first

The venue one-pager asks for **Sat 12pm to Sun 12pm**. The approved Tech Week schedule
runs **Sat 9am to Sun 3:30pm** and Partiful says Sat 9:30am to Sun 2:30pm. If we book a
venue to the one-pager window we end up with an event that runs 2.5 hours past our own
rental. So every email below asks for **Fri Oct 16 8am to Sat Oct 17 4pm, about 32 hours**,
which covers load-in, the approved schedule, and teardown.

That changes the budget math. At 32 hours a $150/hr space is $4,800, not $3,600. The a16z
grant is $5K and it is a reimbursement, so anything above roughly $155/hr does not close
without a venue sponsor or a discount. That is why every email asks for a student rate.

## Open risks

- Every rate above is a list rate off Peerspace. None of it is quoted for a 32 hour
  overnight block, which is not a normal booking, so expect real numbers to differ.
- Skyline Loft requires paid security after 5pm ($50/hr, $75/hr after midnight). Over our
  window that is roughly another $1,500, which pushes it past the grant on its own.
- Angel City's phone number came from a single directory mirror and was not corroborated.
- Only Hot Shot Muffler, Socks Studios, STUDIOS 60 and Open Creative Industrial have
  either a 24 hour calendar or an explicit no-noise-limit policy. The rest need a human
  to say yes to something their booking system does not offer.
