## Description

> MOLTEN is a 24-hour hackathon for prototyping the still-forming tools of spatial
> creation. Spatial consumption shipped this decade, with Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest 3,
> Snap Spectacles all coming out. But spatial creation is unsolved. The tools people use
> to author for these devices are still 2D (Blender + a monitor, Reality Composer,
> Unity + mouse). MOLTEN gives LA's best builders 24 hours to prototype what comes next:
> multimodal authoring tools that let creators make with their whole body.

## The rules

Every submission must:

1. Ship a spatial output — Apple Vision Pro, Meta Quest, Snap Spectacles, WebXR, or
   iOS/Android AR
2. Use at least 2 body-input modalities

No keyboard-only tools.

## Tracks

- **WORLDBUILD** — new tools for authoring 3D environments and scenes
- **NAVIGATE** — new interaction patterns for spatial UIs (menus, timelines, browsers in
  the air)
- **CAPTURE** — new ways to record or generate volumetric content (spatial video,
  holograms, scene reconstruction)
- **INHABIT** — new interfaces for embodying a character or avatar in spatial media
- **OPEN** — any spatial and multimodal creation tool that doesn't fit

## Hardware bench

Submitted as "in confirmation":

- Apple Vision Pro loaners — USC ICT MxR Lab
- Snap Spectacles — Snap AR sponsorship
- Meta Quest 3 — Meta Reality Labs education program

**None of these have been contacted.** See `people.md`.

The application also states a guaranteed floor that does not depend on any of them:
WebXR plus phone AR, so every submission is viable regardless of loaner hardware, backed
by software power-tools like Depth Anything for monocular depth from any camera feed.
That floor is real and is what makes the event survivable if no hardware partner lands.

## Prizes

The application says: **"Prizes: Apple Vision Pro (secured), others tbd."**

The Vision Pro is **not** secured. As of 2026-08-12 a contact of Sissi's was still
negotiating at-cost bulk pricing with Apple. This claim was made to Tech Week and a16z
in the approved application, so it is a commitment, not marketing.

## Who should attend

Current students and recent grads from technical and interdisciplinary backgrounds —
computer science, engineering, HCI, cognitive science, media arts, design.

LA university targets: USC Viterbi and Iovine and Young Academy, UCLA Samueli and DMA,
Caltech CS/CNS, Harvey Mudd, LMU CS, ArtCenter (interaction design), Otis. Also open to
Bay Area students traveling down.

Prior XR experience is welcome but not required — the application promises a pre-event
onboarding kit plus Saturday workshops covering all three entry paths. **Neither the kit
nor the workshops exist yet.**

## Capacity

100–120 hackers plus 20 mentors, judges, and volunteers. Teams of 2–4, yielding 25–30
demos across 5 tracks.

Hardware-heavy tracks (WORLDBUILD, CAPTURE) are sized to loaner units: ~10 Quest,
~5 Vision Pro, ~10 Spectacles. WebXR and phone-AR tracks (NAVIGATE, INHABIT, OPEN) scale
freely with laptops and phones.
