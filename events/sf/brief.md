## Description

> INTERFACE is a 24-hour hackathon that challenges the best devs to reinvent how humans
> interface with machines, the world, and each other.
>
> Every breakthrough in computing has been an interface breakthrough: the mouse, the
> touchscreen, Brain-Computer Interface. We're now living through the biggest interface
> explosion in history, and INTERFACE gives students the hardware, APIs, and creative
> space to define what comes next.

## Tracks

- **NEURAL** — brain-computer interfaces (EEG-controlled applications, neurofeedback,
  brain-state detection)
- **VOICE** — voice-first AI (conversational agents, voice-to-action workflows,
  voice-controlled dev tools)
- **TOUCH** — haptics and vision (gesture control, tactile feedback, VR/AR interactions,
  robotics)
- **VISION** — computer vision and AR (eye-tracking, gaze-based UIs, sign language
  recognition, world models)
- **OPEN** — full-stack software interfaces (reimagined browsers, AI-native tools,
  radical UI/UX)

## Who should attend

Current students and recent grads from technical and interdisciplinary backgrounds — CS,
engineering, neuroscience, design, HCI — with a genuine interest and the agency to make
things work.

## Capacity

80–120 hackers, plus 15–20 mentors, judges, and volunteers. Teams of 2–4, yielding 20–30
demos across 5 tracks.

Hardware tracks are sized to equipment: **~15 EEG headsets and ~10 haptic kits.** We do
not own these and no sponsor has committed them. Motive is the intended source for the
EEG units and has not been contacted. Software tracks (Voice, Vision, Open) scale freely
on laptops and API access.
