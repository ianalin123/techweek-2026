## The build

> INTERFACE is a 24-hour hackathon that challenges the best devs to reinvent how humans
> interface with machines, the world, and each other.
>
> Every breakthrough in computing has been an interface breakthrough: the mouse, the
> touchscreen, the voice assistant. We're now living through the biggest interface
> explosion in history, and INTERFACE gives students the hardware, APIs, and creative
> space to define what comes next.

Note: the approved application said "the mouse, the touchscreen, Brain-Computer
Interface." The one-pager says "the voice assistant." Cosmetic, but the BCI version does
more work for the Neural track.

## The venue ask

**One San Francisco venue.** Open floor space for ~120 builders, tables and power, and
Wi-Fi for 100+ devices. We design the layout around your space.

**Overnight access.** Saturday morning to Sunday evening. Overnight access is best.

### Venue checklist

| Venue provides | Ideal setup | We handle |
|---|---|---|
| Space | Open floor for ~120 hackers | Layout, load-in |
| Power | Tables + abundant outlets | Floor reset |
| Internet | Wi-Fi for ~120 hackers | Usage guidance |
| Access | Saturday morning ~8 AM to Sunday evening ~7 PM | Staffing |
| Operations | Restrooms + load-in access | Catering, cleanup, waivers |

We have a dedicated venue budget and this is a grant-backed student event. We are
seeking a community or partnership rate. Operations are flexible — we can self-manage or
use in-house catering and staffing.

## What sponsors get

- **Billing** — screened Bay Area technical talent, opt-in resume book, recruiting space
  at higher tiers
- **Front-row access** — a judging seat, plus a private walkthrough before the demo expo
  opens
- **Community** — direct access to screened technical talent for recruiting, community,
  or deal flow

### Sponsor rules

- Stage time starts at the Track Sponsor tier
- In-kind value counts at 50% toward cash tiers
- One track per sponsor, first committed first served
