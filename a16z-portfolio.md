# a16z portfolio — speaker, sponsor, and judge targets

Researched 2026-08-26. Nobody here has been contacted.

**Why this list exists.** Alexa's advice was to lock a speaker before scaling sponsor
outreach, and to get there through the a16z relationship rather than cold email. The ask
to the Tech Week grant team converts far better when it names companies than when it asks
open-endedly. This is that list.

**How to use it.** Name two or three for the speaker ask and two or three for the
sponsor ask, separately. Splitting them gives the grant team a way to say yes to one
without turning down the other.

Everything below is verified against a16z's own announcements, a16z Speedrun's company
database, or credible funding coverage. Anything unverified is marked as such. Verify
again before anyone acts on it — this is a snapshot, and portfolio pages move.

### The Austin BCI company — resolved, and the premise was wrong

Alexa offered an intro to the founder of an a16z BCI portfolio company in Austin. No such
company appears to exist. Two got conflated:

| | Austin? | a16z? | Recruiting? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Echo Neurotechnologies** | No — San Francisco | **Yes** | Yes |
| **Paradromics** | **Yes** | No — Prime Movers Lab led | Yes |

Echo is almost certainly who she meant: a16z, founder intro, actively recruiting, all fit.
The city is the detail that slipped. **Worth asking her which she is more confident in,
the city or the investor** — if she really meant Austin she meant Paradromics, the grant
team cannot route us there, and the intro has to come from her directly.

Two near-misses worth not chasing: Neuralink is building in Del Valle outside Austin but
is HQ'd in Fremont with no a16z investment found. Prophetic took money from a16z's *Scout
Fund*, which is not a portfolio relationship and will not route through the grant team.

### Hardware: the portfolio does not solve this

**No a16z portfolio company manufactures a spatial headset we could borrow.** That means
the hardware bench described as "in confirmation" in the approved application still
depends on USC ICT, Snap, and Meta directly. The portfolio offers warm routes, not
substitutes:

- **Flat2VR Studios** and **Trass Games** are Speedrun companies shipping Quest titles
  with live Meta publishing relationships — the realistic warm route to the Meta Reality
  Labs ask.
- **Auto** is the equivalent route to Snap.
- **Anduril** ships EagleEye, a real MR headset built with Meta and Qualcomm, and is
  a16z-backed and in Costa Mesa. They will not lend, and defense is a poor fit for a room
  that includes ArtCenter and Otis students. Noted only so nobody re-derives it later.

### One scheduling note

Speedrun's current cohort SR007 holds its demo day on Oct 6, eleven days before MOLTEN,
and contains nothing spatial — it is enterprise, AI agents, and health. If the grant team
defaults to offering the current cohort, that is the wrong cohort. The spatial companies
are in SR001 through SR004.

## What is not verified

- a16z's main portfolio page is JavaScript-rendered and could not be enumerated, so the
  main-fund names here were each verified individually rather than swept exhaustively.
  There are likely good names missing, particularly in TOUCH.
- **Recruiting posture is mostly inferred from stage and funding**, not confirmed against
  careers pages. Only Echo, Refactor, Uthana, and Intangible were checked directly. This
  matters — a company hiring new grads has a concrete reason to sponsor a student
  hackathon and one that isn't doesn't. [jobs.a16z.com](https://jobs.a16z.com/) is a16z's
  own portfolio job board and is the fast way to confirm before naming anyone.
- Echo does not appear on a16z's public investment list, which is normal for stealth and
  Bio+Health deals. Its membership rests on press coverage, not a16z's own page.
- Checked and **not** a16z, despite fitting the theme: Polycam, Genies, Decart,
  Paradromics.
